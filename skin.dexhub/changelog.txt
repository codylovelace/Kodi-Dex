3.19.1.1
- Kodi-Dex build: published from the Kodi-Dex repository (repository.kodidex), github.com/codylovelace/Kodi-Dex. Unchanged from upstream 3.19.1.

3.19.1
- The colourful badge images are gone: Arctic Fuse 3's colorful flags (media/af3/flags/colorful) and the badges trimmed from them (media/dexhub/badges) are replaced by Dex Hub's own framed white words, same names; the style is called Framed.
- Installs in any order with Dex Hub: the import asks for any Dex Hub 5.10 (Kodi refused 3.19.0, which asked for 5.10.141, when its zip was installed first, and 3.18 stayed with its grey plates).
- A pack's or the folder's row shows once the add-on has named the first badge (width class or word); until then (an older Dex Hub) the colourful badges show.

3.19.0
- Player badges: the Elite pack (official logos), Gold and Minimalist white, downloaded once and kept on the device; each image drawn at one height in a box as wide as its shape, cut to its drawing (no grey plate).
- SVG badges (Kodi cannot draw them) shown as framed words in the pack's colours.
- Even gaps: the empty width beside each drawing (sent by the add-on per slot) is taken back from the gap.
- Badges from your folder: the skin's badges unless the folder has an image of the same name.
- Badge page: every choice with a live preview; the chosen one ticked; folder options.
- Unfocused rows on the Home and collection pages fade to 85 percent (was 60, which looked grey); setting in Home and widgets: light, none or strong.
- Badge page texts in Dex Hub's language (Close included); works best with Dex Hub 5.10.141 or later.

3.18.0
- Player info overlays back to the 3.14 (Arctic Fuse 3) layout: the work logo above the details,
  plot and the bottom line, with Dex Hub's ratings and their logos added to that line.
- Network/studio logo without a plate, at the right end of the bottom line (white pack first).
- Work logo "top left" / "top right" drawn at fixed places (3.17 used $VAR positions Kodi ignores);
  "logo off" shows the title in words.
- Colourful badges: Arctic Fuse 3's clear flags, trimmed and sized per badge (media/dexhub/badges),
  no tint, first match per group: source, resolution, HDR/Dolby Vision, codec, audio, channels.
  Fixes 4K/8K and 3D images that never loaded (case and paths).
- Badges show unless their style is Off (no longer hidden behind the ExtraOSD option).
- Minimal and compact styles name things plainly (4K, 1080p, HDR10, DD+ Atmos, 5.1).
- Player & Quality settings: "Details over the player controls" toggle, pack import moved to
  the badges page, AF3 player options last; one help line (two texts overlapped).
- Dialog frames: shadow outside the dialog, so its edges are no longer see-through.
- Pair with Dex Hub 5.10.140.

3.16.1

  letters (such as HD, MAX and UHD), superscripts, arrows and common symbols.

  This is not a complete colour emoji or multi-codepoint flag renderer.
